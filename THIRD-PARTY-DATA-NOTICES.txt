Open English WordNet
--------------------
EL Bot materializes Open English WordNet for English lexical forms, senses,
synonyms, morphology, and taxonomy/hypernym relations.
Project: Open English WordNet
Source: https://en-word.net/
Phase 6 Step 1 target edition: Open English WordNet 2025+ (2025 edition plus the
curated Open English Namenet proper-name extension).
Open English WordNet is derived from Princeton WordNet by the Open English WordNet
Community and is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0) License.
